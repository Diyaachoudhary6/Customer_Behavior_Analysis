# Customer Shopping Behavior Analysis

## 📌 Project Overview

The goal of this project is to simulate a corporate-grade end-to-end data analytics workflow, demonstrating the ability to translate raw data into strategic business intelligence by:

✅ Data Preparation,Modeling & Exploratory Data Analysis (Python): Clean and transform the raw dataset for analysis.

✅ Data Analysis (SQL): Simulate business transactions, and run queries to extract insights on customer segments, loyalty, and purchase drivers.

✅ Visualization & Insights (Power BI): Build an interactive dashboard that highlights key patterns and trends, enabling stakeholders to make data-driven decisions.

✅ Report and Presentation: Write a clear project report summarizing your key findings and business recommendations. Prepare a presentation that visually communicates insights and actionable recommendations to stakeholders.

This project analyzes customer shopping behavior using transactional data from **3,900 purchases across 18 columns**. The objective is to identify spending patterns, customer segments, product preferences, subscription behavior, and purchase drivers to support data-driven business decisions.

The project follows a complete end-to-end data analytics workflow:

**Python → Data Cleaning & EDA → PostgreSQL → SQL Analysis → Power BI Dashboard → Business Recommendations**

The analysis addresses the business question:

> **How can the company leverage consumer shopping data to identify trends, improve customer engagement, and optimize marketing and product strategies?**

---

## 🎯 Business Problem

A retail company wants to better understand its customers' shopping behavior to improve:

* Sales performance
* Customer satisfaction
* Customer engagement
* Customer loyalty
* Marketing effectiveness
* Product positioning
* Subscription adoption

The analysis focuses on understanding how factors such as **discounts, reviews, demographics, shipping preferences, purchasing behavior, and subscription status** influence customer decisions and revenue.

## 2. Data Cleaning & Preparation

The dataset was cleaned and prepared for further analysis.

### Missing Value Treatment

There were **37 missing values in the Review Rating column**.

Missing review ratings were imputed using the **median rating of the respective product category**.

### Column Standardization

Column names were standardized using **snake_case** naming conventions to improve readability and consistency.

### Feature Engineering

Two additional analytical features were created:

* `age_group`
* `purchase_frequency_days`

### Data Consistency

The relationship between `discount_applied` and `promo_code_used` was examined. Since the fields were considered redundant for the analysis, `promo_code_used` was removed.

# 🔎 4. SQL Business Analysis

The PostgreSQL database was used to answer **10 key business questions**.

### Q1. Revenue by Gender

Compared total revenue generated by male and female customers.

```sql
SELECT gender, SUM(purchase_amount) AS revenue
FROM customer
GROUP BY gender;
```

### Q2. High-Spending Discount Users

Identified customers who used discounts while spending at or above the average purchase amount.

### Q3. Top 5 Products by Rating

Identified products with the highest average customer review ratings.

### Q4. Shipping Type Comparison

Compared average purchase amounts between Standard and Express shipping customers.

### Q5. Subscribers vs. Non-Subscribers

Compared:

* Number of customers
* Average spending
* Total revenue

between subscribers and non-subscribers.

### Q6. Discount-Dependent Products

Identified the five products with the highest percentage of purchases involving discounts.

### Q7. Customer Segmentation

Customers were classified into three segments based on previous purchases:

| Segment   | Previous Purchases |
| --------- | -----------------: |
| New       |                  1 |
| Returning |               2–10 |
| Loyal     |                >10 |

The SQL analysis used a `CASE` statement to create these customer segments.

### Q8. Top 3 Products by Category

Used a SQL window function to rank products within each category and identify the three most purchased products.

### Q9. Repeat Buyers & Subscriptions

Analyzed whether customers with more than five previous purchases were more likely to have a subscription.

### Q10. Revenue by Age Group

Calculated the number of customers and total revenue contribution of each age group.

# 💡 Key Insights

### 1. Gender-Based Revenue

Female customers generated **slightly higher total revenue than male customers**, suggesting an opportunity for gender-specific marketing strategies.

### 2. High-Value Discount Customers

The analysis identified customers who use discounts while still spending above the average purchase amount. These customers represent an opportunity for targeted premium offers.

### 3. Top-Rated Products

Products such as **Blouse, Dress, and Shirt** demonstrated strong customer ratings and satisfaction.

### 4. Express Shipping

Express-shipping customers had an average purchase amount of approximately **$65**, compared with approximately **$58** for Standard shipping.

This represents approximately **12% higher spending per transaction** among Express-shipping customers.

### 5. Subscription Behavior

Subscribers demonstrated substantially higher spending and contributed a significant share of revenue. The project presentation reports:

* **68% higher spend**
* **45% revenue share**
* **78% loyalty rate**

### 6. Customer Segmentation

The customer base was segmented into:

| Segment   | Share |
| --------- | ----: |
| Loyal     |   15% |
| Returning |   35% |
| New       |   50% |

A key opportunity is to move customers from:

**New → Returning → Loyal**.

# 📌 Business Recommendations

Based on the analysis, the following strategies were recommended:

### 🔹 1. Boost Subscription Adoption

Promote exclusive benefits and incentives to increase subscription adoption and strengthen customer retention.

### 🔹 2. Strengthen Loyalty Programs

Reward repeat customers and create strategies to move customers from the **New** segment into **Returning** and eventually **Loyal** customers.

### 🔹 3. Review Discount Strategy

Identify products and customers that are highly dependent on discounts and balance promotional activity with profitability considerations.

### 🔹 4. Improve Product Positioning

Highlight highly rated and best-selling products in marketing campaigns and promotional activities.

### 🔹 5. Target High-Value Customers

Focus marketing efforts on high-revenue customer segments and customers showing higher spending behavior, including Express-shipping users.
